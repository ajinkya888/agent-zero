### reflect:
Explicitly pause to analyze a complex situation, critique your own plan, or brainstorm solutions.
Does not perform any external action. Use this when you are stuck or before making a major decision.

usage:
~~~json
{
    "thoughts": [
        "The current approach is failing because...",
    ],
    "headline": "Analyzing complex failure state",
    "tool_name": "reflect",
    "tool_args": {
        "thoughts": "Detailed analysis of why the previous steps didn't work and what needs to change."
    }
}
~~~
